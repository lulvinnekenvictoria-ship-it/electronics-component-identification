## Exercise 1 – Component Identification

### IC NE555D Timer SMD
* **Description:** A popular timer IC in a surface mount SO-8 package with precise timing capabilities (astable or monostable mode).
* **Pinout:** Pin 1 on the left; numbering runs counter-clockwise.
* **Citation:** Digikey (2024)

### LED 3mm Red (1.85V High Intensity)
* **Description:** High-intensity red indicator LED operating at 1.85V forward voltage.
* **Polarity:** Anode (+), Cathode (-)
* **Citation:** Rapid Education (2024)

### Capacitor Electrolytic 4.7µF 63V
* **Description:** Polarized energy-storage capacitor used for power filtering, decoupling, and timing.
* **Polarity:** Cathode pin (-)
* **Citation:** Switch Electronics (2024)

### Capacitor Polyester 100nF
* **Description:** Non-polarized capacitor for signal processing, filtering, and decoupling.
* **Citation:** Rapid Education (2024)

### Diode 1N4148
* **Description:** High-speed switching diode rated up to 100V reverse voltage and 200mA forward current.
* **Polarity:** Anode (+), Cathode (-)
* **Citation:** Switch Electronics (2024)

### Trimmer Potentiometer 50kΩ (Bourns 9mm)
* **Description:** Variable resistor for voltage division, tuning, and calibration.
* **Pinout:** 3-pin configuration (outer pins define total resistance; middle pin is variable wiper).
* **Citation:** Rapid Education (2024)

### Resistor 4k7 ¼W 1% Axial
* **Description:** Through-hole fixed resistor for current limiting and pull-up/down circuits.
* **Polarity:** Non-polarized.
* **Citation:** Mouser Electronics (2024)

### Resistor 8k2 1206 1% 1/8W SMD
* **Description:** Surface-mount chip resistor for high-density automotive and consumer electronics.
* **Polarity:** Non-polarized.
* **Citation:** Rapid Education (2024)

### RJ45 Panel Mount Socket
* **Description:** 8-pin modular female Ethernet connector for panel/enclosure mounting.
* **Citation:** RS Online (2024)

### Transistor BC546
* **Description:** NPN Bipolar Junction Transistor (BJT) in TO-92 packaging for low-signal amplification and switching.
* **Pinout:** Collector (C), Base (B), Emitter (E)
* **Citation:** Mouser Electronics (2024)
